RESEARCH BRIEF — Khmer calendar event start years & conventions
Context: A Cambodian calendar data catalog tracks observances with a "first
observance year" and, for anniversaries, a "count-from year" (anniversaryBase).
Cambodian calendars print anniversary counts like "47th" (ខួបលើកទី47).
For each question, answer with: the exact YEAR (and date if relevant), whether
the year refers to Cambodia's own first observance or the international one,
and a source (prefer Royal Government of Cambodia sub-decrees/anukret, official
holiday lists from the Ministry of Labour or National Bank of Cambodia, LRC
lunar calendar publications, or the founding UN/UNESCO resolution).
