**Priority 1 — International Observances Clamped at 2000**

* **World Health Day (7 April)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1950** (7 April 1950).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** First World Health Assembly (1948) chose 7 April to commemorate the coming into force of the WHO Constitution.


* **World Red Cross and Red Crescent Day (8 May)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1948** (8 May 1948).
* **Scope:** International (Cambodian Red Cross was founded on 18 February 1955, but CRC holds its primary national fundraising gala annually on 8 May).
* **Source:** International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC) / Resolution of the XIVth International Conference (1934/1948).


* **International Literacy Day (8 September)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1967** (8 September 1967).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** UNESCO General Conference 14th Session (Resolution 1.441, 26 October 1966).


* **World Tourism Day (27 September)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1980** (27 September 1980).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** UN World Tourism Organization (UNWTO) General Assembly, Torremolinos, Spain (September 1979).


* **World AIDS Day (1 December)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1988** (1 December 1988).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** World Health Organization Global Programme on AIDS / UN General Assembly Resolution 43/15.


* **World Tuberculosis Day (24 March)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1982** (Commemorative centenary of Koch's discovery) / **1996** (Official WHO-promoted annual campaign). Cambodian calendars count the awareness initiative from **1982**.
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** IUATLD (1982) / WHO Resolution WHA46.36.


* **World No Tobacco Day (31 May)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1989** (The initial 1988 event was held on 7 April; resolution WHA42.19 moved it permanently to 31 May starting in 1989).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** World Health Assembly Resolution WHA42.19 (1988).


* **World Teachers' Day (5 October)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1994** (5 October 1994).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** UNESCO (inaugurated in 1994 to commemorate the 1966 UNESCO/ILO Recommendation).


* **International Day of Families (15 May)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1994** (15 May 1994).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** UN General Assembly Resolution A/RES/47/237 (20 September 1993).


* **International Day of the World's Indigenous Peoples (9 August)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1995** (9 August 1995; resolution passed in December 1994).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** UN General Assembly Resolution A/RES/49/214 (23 December 1994).


* **International Day of Persons with Disabilities (3 December)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1992** (3 December 1992).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** UN General Assembly Resolution A/RES/47/3 (14 October 1992).


* **International Day Against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking (26 June)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1988** (26 June 1988).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** UN General Assembly Resolution A/RES/42/112 (7 December 1987).


* **International Civil Aviation Day (7 December)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1994** (50th anniversary of the 1944 Chicago Convention; officially endorsed by the UNGA in 1996).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** ICAO Assembly Resolution A29-1 (1992) / UNGA Resolution A/RES/51/33 (1996).


* **International Day of Peace (21 September)**
* **Model pre-2002 as a floating date:** From **1982 through 2001**, it was held on the opening day of the UN General Assembly session (the **3rd Tuesday of September** per UNGA Res 36/67). The fixed date of **21 September** applies strictly starting in **2002** (per UNGA Res 55/282).


* **National Day of Remembrance (20 May)**
* **Origin Year:** **1984** (Instituted on 20 May 1984 as the "Day of Hatred" / *ទិវាចងកំហឹង* under the People's Republic of Kampuchea to mark the start of mass killings under Democratic Kampuchea on 20 May 1975).
* **Modern Revival:** **2018** (Renamed and codified as an official national commemoration day via Sub-Decree No. 19 ANKr.BK, dated 14 February 2018).
* **Scope:** Cambodia.

**Priority 2 — Cambodia-Specific Observances**

* **Queen Mother's Birthday (18 June)**
* **First Observance Year:** **1994** (Recognized following the restoration of the Monarchy in 1993; formal title updated from Queen to Queen Mother in 2004).
* **Scope:** Cambodia.
* **Source:** Royal Decree / Annual RGC Holiday Sub-Decrees since 1994.


* **Arbor Day / Rukkha Divas (9 July)**
* **Origin Year:** **1952** (Instituted by King Norodom Sihanouk during the Sangkum Reastr Niyum; formally revived post-civil war by Royal Decree in **1989** and anchored in the 2002 Law on Forestry).
* **Scope:** Cambodia.
* **Source:** Law on Forestry (2002), Article 60.


* **National Population Day (11 July)**
* **Origin Year:** **1999** (Aligned with UN World Population Day following the completion and release of Cambodia's first modern General Population Census of 1998).
* **Scope:** Cambodia.
* **Source:** Directive of the Council of Ministers / Ministry of Planning (1999).


* **National Clean Water Day (4 March)**
* **Origin Year:** **2023** (4 March 2023).
* **Scope:** Cambodia.
* **Source:** Sub-Decree (*Anukret*) No. 31 ANKr.BK (dated 14 February 2023).


* **National Mine Awareness Day (24 February)**
* **Origin & Count Base:** Marks the date in 1999 when Prime Minister Hun Sen declared a nationwide ban on landmines and initiated the national awareness campaign. First officially observed on **24 February 2000**.
* **Anniversary Base:** **1999** (e.g., 2024 = 25th observance; 2026 = 27th observance).
* **Source:** Sub-Decree of the Royal Government of Cambodia (signed late 1999).


* **Cambodian Older Persons Day (1 October)**
* **Origin Year:** **2017** (Established specifically as a distinct Cambodian national observance merged with the UN day via Sub-Decree No. 248 ANKr.BK, dated 23 October 2017). Prior to 2017, Cambodia observed it informally via UN designation.
* **Scope:** Cambodia.


* **National and World Environment Day (5 June)**
* **Origin & Base:** Cambodia established its own "National Environment Day" via Sub-Decree in **1996**, anchored on World Environment Day (5 June).
* **Counting Convention:** Printed Cambodian calendars count the *national* celebration from base year **1996**, while referring to the global milestone (1974).
* **Source:** Sub-Decree on the Establishment of National Environment Day (1996).


* **Win-Win Policy Day vs. Peace Day (29 December)**
* **Win-Win Policy Day (1999–2023):** Commemorated the complete dissolution of the Khmer Rouge on 29 December 1998. It was an official state commemorative day, but *not* a paid public holiday.
* **Peace Day (2024 onward):** Replaced Win-Win Day as an official **paid public holiday** beginning on **29 December 2024**.
* **Source:** Sub-Decree (*Anukret*) No. 01 ANKr.BK (dated 1 January 2024).


* **National Anti-Human Trafficking Day (12 December)**
* **Origin Year:** **2007** (First observed on 12 December 2007; established by the RGC in collaboration with the National Committee for Counter Trafficking).
* **Scope:** Cambodia.
* **Source:** RGC Decision / Ministry of Interior Directive (2007).


* **Verification of Specific Start Years:**
* *National Environmental Sanitation Day (23 Nov):* **1995** (First created by Sub-Decree No. 63 ANKr.BK, 23 November 1995; reaffirmed by Ministry of Environment in 2017).
* *National Fish Day (1 July):* **2002** (Established via Sub-Decree by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries; first released fingerling ceremonies held 1 July 2003).
* *National Maternal, Newborn, and Child Health Day (21 Feb):* **2012** (Established by the Council of Ministers in 2012; first observance 21 February 2013).
* *National Authors' Day (7 Dec):* **2024** (Established by Prime Minister Hun Manet; first observed 7 December 2024).
* *International Buddhist Day (8 April):* **2014** (Proclaimed internationally at the 6th World Buddhist Summit; Cambodia ratified and added it to the official domestic calendar in **2024**). Unlike Vesak (which commemorates the Buddha's Birth, Enlightenment, and Parinirvana on the lunar full moon of Visakha), 8 April represents the secular calendar date recognized by the World Buddhist Supreme Conference.


* **International Anti-Corruption Day (9 December)**
* **First Observance Year:** **2004** (9 December 2004).
* **Scope:** International.
* **Source:** UN General Assembly Resolution 58/4 (adopted 31 October 2003, designating 9 December as the annual day).

**World Water Day (22 Mar) vs. World Meteorological Day (23 Mar)**

* **Catalog Recommendation:** **Split into two separate events.**
* **Reasoning:**
* Astronomically and under UN mandates, they are distinct: World Water Day is 22 March (UNGA 1993); World Meteorological Day is 23 March (WMO Convention 1950).
* In Cambodia, the Ministry of Water Resources and Meteorology (MOWRAM) frequently holds a single, joint commemorative ceremony on either 22 or 23 March to conserve administrative resources.
* Standard Khmer wall calendars (including Buddhist Institute releases) print them on their respective distinct dates (**22 March** for Water, **23 March** for Meteorology).
